## Lab 02 — Agile Backlog Creation & Sprint Simulation in Jira
 
**Problem Statement:** Parking Reservation & Bay Management
 
Lab 02 focuses on Agile backlog creation and sprint simulation using Jira. The lab includes creating epics and user stories, estimating story points, prioritizing the backlog, planning and simulating a sprint, and managing the Jira workflow.
 
The sprint simulation follows the workflow **To Do → In Progress → Done**. The lab also includes Jira reports and a burndown chart to analyze sprint progress and team capacity, along with reflection on estimation, backlog prioritization, sprint execution, and sprint performance.
